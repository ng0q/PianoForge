# Audio transcription research

## Goal

Identify, evaluate, and prepare a suitable Automatic Music Transcription (AMT) model for integration into PianoForge. The work should produce a reproducible baseline, a record of model quality and computational requirements, and a decision on whether adaptation or fine-tuning is needed.
